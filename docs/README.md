## Evidence and history

Registered protocols, result CSV/JSON, selections, decisions and hash/provenance records remain at their original `results/`, `reports/`, `config/`, `experiments/` and `benchmarks/` paths. They are scientific records, including unsuccessful and invalidated attempts; their numerical contents are not rewritten for presentation.

[Scientific progression](experiments/history.md) provides orientation. [History](history/README.md) groups development narratives and superseded proposals; [archives](../archives/README.md) indexes raw recovery. `cleanup/` and `reports/repository_cleanup/` are dated retention/provenance audits, not current project status. The older top-level architecture/installation/methodology/reproducibility/history URLs are compatibility entry points into this hierarchy.
