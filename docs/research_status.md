# Current research status

**Frozen under the original tested hypothesis.** Gate 10A's registered classification is `PACT_GATE10A_NO_MATERIAL_PHYSICAL_IMPACT`; its development recommendation is `FREEZE`.

PACT demonstrated legal scan-order optimization, independent replay and routed wire/activity tradeoffs. Nine frozen b14/b15 architectures qualified in the final study, but no comparison met the material static-PI criterion. Primary worst-static-drop improvements were 3 µV and 8 µV. Thermal impact was not evaluated; transient droop was not established.

Read the [public summary](../README.md), [negative findings](experiments/negative_results.md), [evidence index](reproduction/canonical_results.md) and [final report/decision](../reports/gate10a/report.md). These define the current state without reconstructing older milestones.
